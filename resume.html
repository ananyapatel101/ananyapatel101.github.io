2. SAP S/4HANA FICO End-to-End Implementation: XYZ Pvt Ltd

Self project · Sep 2026, during SAP FICO training (AppliSAP) · One fictional company code (IN10) with figures tied across every module

  • Financial accounting setup: Configured the company code from scratch (ledger, April–March fiscal year, posting periods, document types, number ranges, user tolerance limits), so entries in closed months and postings above a user's approval limit are blocked; 12 test cases prove each control.
    • Documented every setting in a configuration workbook, with the reason for each value, ready for sign-off.
  • Procure-to-pay (MM–FI): Ran a purchase from requisition to a ₹6,84,400 payment run (F110), with automatic account postings (OBYC), a three-way match and GST input credit, so the company pays only for goods received, at the agreed price, and only once.
    • 12 test cases cover price and quantity blocks, duplicate invoices and payment due dates.
  • Order-to-cash (SD–FI): Ran a sale from sales order with a credit check to delivery, a GST invoice and customer payment with a 2% early-payment discount and the customer's TDS, so credit-limit breaches are stopped and short payments are cleared correctly.
    • 12 test cases cover credit blocks, stock availability, billing errors and payment tolerances.
  • Asset accounting (FI-AA): Set up Companies Act and Income Tax Act depreciation, capitalised a ₹21.60 lakh machine through assets under construction, sold an old lathe at a ₹44,000 profit and ran monthly depreciation (AFAB).
    • 12 test cases cover posting periods, tolerances and the year-end close; the 11-asset register ties to the ledger.
  • Month-end close and reporting: Built a 38-account chart of accounts and a Schedule III financial statement version, then closed September with six accruals and deferrals (FBS1, reversed with F.81); without them profit would have been overstated by ₹2.74 lakh. Results shown in a Power BI dashboard.
    • Interactive statements let a reviewer switch each accrual on or off and watch the Balance Sheet, P&L and ratios recompute.
  • One set of books: The goods-received-not-invoiced balance (₹3.40 lakh), customer balances (₹64.80 lakh) and the 11-asset register all tie to the 30 Sep 2026 Balance Sheet and P&L. Full write-ups and live simulators are in the online portfolio.
