SAP FI · General Ledger

Financial Statement Version & Month-End Close

Built a 38-account chart of accounts (FS00) and a Schedule III FSV (OB58) that produces a balancing Balance Sheet and P&L. Then ran the September close: six FBS1 accruals and deferrals, reversed on day 1 with F.81. The close lowers PAT by ₹2.74 lakh, and the current ratio moves from 2.68 to 2.56.
