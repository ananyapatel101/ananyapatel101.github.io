Asset Accounting (FI-AA)

I set up Asset Accounting for company code IN10 and ran a full asset month. The setup covers a chart of depreciation with a book area (Companies Act) and a tax area (Income Tax Act), asset classes, depreciation keys and AO90 account determination. In October a new machine was bought into an asset under construction, installed and capitalised, an old lathe was sold at a profit, and the depreciation run posted. The eleven assets in the register add up exactly to the fixed assets and depreciation in Part 5's trial balance, and a 12-case unit-test script proves each control.

Scenario

One asset month, five postings

XYZ Inc. (IN10) adds a vertical machining centre, the VMC-1060, to its Pune plant. The machine comes from Bharat CNC Machines in Bengaluru and is installed by a local firm, so its costs collect on an asset under construction (AUC) until it is ready for use on 20 October. The same month an old manual lathe is sold to a used-machinery dealer, and on 31 October the depreciation run posts. All data is fictional.

Blue lines are debits and orange lines are credits. Line 70/75 postings to an asset number land on the G/L account from AO90: 4000001 on 119000, 1100008 and 1100002 on 111000 and 111900.

Rule · FI-AA integration

  1. Recon accounts, not plain G/L accounts. 111000–112900 are reconciliation accounts for account type A. Nobody can post a manual journal to them, so the trial balance can't drift away from the asset register. The asset register must add up to the G/L at every month-end (it does: see the register).
  2. FSV before the first AUC posting. Part 5's PPE item covers 0000111000–0000119999, so 119000 would show as property, plant and equipment. Schedule III shows capital work-in-progress as its own line. Following rule INS3-01, I cut the PPE range to 0000111000–0000118999 and added item (b) Capital work-in-progress (BS.A.NCA.CWIP) for 0000119000–0000119999, then re-ran the check: 0 not assigned. 219700, 423000 and 665000 already fall inside existing ranges.
  3. Capitalise net of GST. Input GST on capital goods is claimed in full as credit (161000). Section 16(3) of the CGST Act doesn't allow credit if depreciation is also claimed on the tax part, so the asset value is ₹21,60,000, not ₹25,48,800.

F-92 · ABAVN · GST on used assets

Selling the lathe: NBV, profit and GST

  1. NBVThe lathe cost ₹7,20,000 in April 2021 and has been depreciated for 66 months at ₹4,000: ₹2,64,000. Period control 01 for retirements means no depreciation in the month of sale, so NBV on 26.10 is ₹4,56,000.
  2. ProfitSale value ₹5,00,000 − NBV ₹4,56,000 = ₹44,000 profit to 423000. SAP works this out itself from the asset values; the user enters only the customer, the sale value (to clearing account 219700) and the asset.
  3. GSTSection 18(6) of the CGST Act: on selling a used capital good, pay the higher of the credit taken less 5% per quarter of use, or tax on the sale value. After 22 quarters the first is nil, so GST is 18% of ₹5,00,000 (buyer in Maharashtra: CGST + SGST ₹45,000 each).
  4. Tax blockFor income tax, the plant & machinery block is reduced by the ₹5,00,000 sale price, not by the NBV. The ₹44,000 book profit isn't taxed as such.

Scrapping instead (AA-03, photocopiers 1200003, ABAVN): no customer and no revenue, so the whole NBV of ₹2,50,000 goes to 665000 as a loss. A machine sold as scrap would add TCS under 206C(1); a working machine sold as a machine doesn't.

Business impact

Why asset controls matter

Fixed assets are IN10's largest asset class: ₹1.51 crore of net book value, 45% of total assets in Part 5's balance sheet. Depreciation of ₹1.7 lakh a month is the biggest non-cash cost in the P&L. With recon accounts and an asset register that agrees to the ledger, the auditor can trace every rupee of PPE to a numbered asset with a location, a capitalisation date and a useful life. The AUC keeps a half-installed machine out of depreciation until it can produce, and the "put to use" date moves the first-year tax deduction by ₹1.62 lakh. Closing the asset year with AJAB after the audit keeps the signed numbers from changing.

Interview guide

How I present it in 90 seconds

  2. Configuration: "Chart of depreciation IN10 from 1IN, with book area 01 posting to the ledger and tax area 15 for the Income Tax Act. Asset classes carry the AO90 account determination, number range and depreciation keys."
  3. Controls: "The asset accounts are recon accounts, so nobody can post to them by hand. AUCs don't depreciate, OB52 and AJAB guard the dates, and OBA4 decides who can post a ₹24.78 lakh capital invoice."
  4. Proof: "Twelve unit tests and a simulator. And it ties back: my asset register adds up to the fixed assets and the ₹9.9 lakh of depreciation in the trial balance of my month-end close (Part 5)."
What is a depreciation area?

A separate valuation of the same asset. Area 01 is the book value under the Companies Act and posts to the G/L; area 15 holds Income Tax Act values without posting. Each area has its own key, useful life and values.

Why use an asset under construction?

While a machine is being installed, its costs aren't depreciable yet. Posting them to an AUC (depreciation key 0000, shown as capital work-in-progress) collects them in one place. IN10's AUC class manages line items, so when the machine is ready for use I enter a distribution rule in AIAB and settle with AIBU; depreciation on the final asset starts from that date. An AUC without line items would be moved with a transfer (ABUMN) instead.

What does AO90 control?

For each account determination key it gives the G/L accounts for APC, accumulated depreciation, depreciation expense, and gains, losses and clearing on retirement. The asset class points to the key.

How is the gain or loss on a sale calculated?

Sale value minus NBV on the value date. SAP retires the APC and accumulated depreciation (with depreciation up to the value date according to period control), compares them with the revenue and posts the difference to the gain or loss account.

What happens in the year-end asset close?

AJRW opens the new asset fiscal year and carries values forward; it runs at the start of the year. AJAB closes the old year after the audit, so no posting can change it. Before AJAB: all AFAB runs posted, no AUC settlements pending, and the history sheet reconciled with the G/L.

Why can't you post to 111000 with FB50?

It's a reconciliation account for account type A. Postings must come from Asset Accounting with an asset number, which keeps the sub-ledger and the G/L equal.
