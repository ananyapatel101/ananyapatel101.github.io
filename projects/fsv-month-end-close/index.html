Deliverable 1b

SOP B: Build the Financial Statement Version in OB58

  1. OB58New entries: FSV INS3, name "Schedule III – Companies Act 2013", maintenance language EN, item keys automatic, chart of accounts INCA, group account number off.
  2. OB58Select INS3 → Financial statement items. SAP creates the root plus the special items: Assets, Liabilities & equity, Net result: profit, Net result: loss, P&L result and Not assigned.
  3. OB58Rename the top items to Schedule III wording, then build sub-items (Create items, same level or sub-level) down to note level, such as "(c) Other current liabilities".
  4. OB58On each end item, click Assign accounts and enter From and To accounts using full account numbers. Tick D and C to show the account at this item whatever its balance.
  5. OB58D/C shift for HDFC 171000: D only under Cash & cash equivalents and C only under Short-term borrowings. An overdraft then moves to liabilities automatically.
  6. OB58Net result: place the "Net result: profit / loss" item under Reserves & surplus, so the current-period P&L appears in equity and the Balance Sheet balances before year-end carryforward.
  7. OB58Check assignments (Goto → Check). The result must show no accounts under Not assigned and no account assigned twice for the same D/C side.
  8. S_ALR_87012284Run the statement: company code IN10, FSV INS3, FY 2026, periods 01–06, comparison FY 2025. In Fiori, use the Balance Sheet/Income Statement app.

Deliverable 2

Configuration rule: FSV integrity

Rule INS3-01 · applies to every FSV change

  1. Assign by full account number. Every account goes on an FSV item as an explicit From–To range of full 10-digit internal numbers (0000131000–0000139999). Never use a truncated or partial string such as 131 or 13*. A range that doesn't match exactly drops the account into Not assigned, and the Balance Sheet stops balancing without any error message.
  2. Protect history. Once an account has postings, never delete or renumber the FSV item it rolls up to. If an account must move, reassign it with a dated change note, and re-run prior-period statements to confirm they still reproduce the figures already reported.
  3. Check before every close. Run OB58 → Check assignments before the period is closed. The only acceptable result is 0 accounts under Not assigned and 0 accounts assigned twice for the same debit/credit side.

Why it holds up with auditors: the FSV is a reporting view, not a posting object. History lives in the documents and account balances. The FSV only groups them, so the audit trail is protected by keeping the account-to-item mapping complete, unambiguous and version-controlled. The live statements page runs this check on every refresh.

Business impact

Why this matters

The FSV is the link between thousands of G/L postings and the two statements that the board, auditors and lenders actually read. If it's built to Schedule III with complete, full-number account ranges, the statutory Balance Sheet and P&L come straight out of SAP, with no manual regrouping in Excel. Every figure can be drilled back to the source document.

Accruals and deferrals apply the matching principle: expenses and income land in the month they belong to, not the month the paper arrives. Posting them with FBS1 and reversing them with F.81 makes the process repeatable and leaves an audit trail, and it can't be forgotten, because every reversal is scheduled when the accrual is posted. The result is a faster close, reliable monthly margins and fewer audit adjustments.

How I present it in 90 seconds

  2. Reporting: "I mapped them into a Schedule III FSV in OB58 using full account ranges. That includes a D/C shift, so a bank overdraft moves to short-term borrowings, and a net-result node so the balance sheet balances mid-year."
  3. Close: "For September I posted four accruals and two deferrals in FBS1 with a reversal date, then reversed them on day 1 with F.81. The close reduced PAT by ₹2.74 lakh and the current ratio from 2.68 to 2.56."
  4. Control: "Before every close I run the OB58 assignment check and make sure nothing sits in Not assigned. The live statements page shows the same check."
Why FBS1 and not FB50 for an accrual?

FBS1 stores the reversal date and reason on the document, so F.81 picks up every open accrual automatically. With FB50, someone has to remember to reverse each one by hand.

What if the actual bill differs from the accrual?

The difference lands in the next period, here ₹3,500 in October. If variances are consistently large, the estimation basis (meter reading × tariff) gets reviewed.

Accrual versus deferral?

An accrual records something that has happened but isn't billed yet (power used, interest earned). A deferral pushes something already billed or received into the period it relates to (October rent, October subscription).

How does the P&L reach the Balance Sheet?

During the year, the FSV's net result item shows current profit inside Reserves & surplus. At year-end, FAGLGVTR carries P&L accounts forward to retained earnings 321000, as set in OB53.

What does D/C shift do?

It lets one account appear on different FSV items depending on its balance. HDFC 171000 is an asset when it has a debit balance and a short-term borrowing when overdrawn.

What happens if an account isn't in the FSV?

Its balance goes to Not assigned, the statement no longer balances and nothing flags it during posting. That's why the assignment check is part of the close checklist.
