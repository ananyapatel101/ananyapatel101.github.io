Order-to-Cash (O2C)

I set up and ran the full sales cycle for company code IN10: a sales order with a credit check, a delivery and goods issue, a GST invoice from billing, and an incoming payment with cash discount and the customer's TDS. The work covers the sales org structure, pricing, VKOA revenue account determination, SAP Credit Management and payment tolerances, and a 12-case unit-test script proves each control. The customer open items behind it add up to the ₹64,80,000 of trade receivables in Part 5's trial balance.

Scenario

One sale, five documents, three ledger entries

Kalinga Auto Components (customer 200001, Cuttack, Odisha) orders 1,000 brake drums (FG-2001) from XYZ Pvt Ltd (IN10) at ₹2,000 each, less its 3% customer discount. The Pune plant (IN11) ships them on 5 October, billing raises a GST invoice the same day, and the customer pays nine days later, taking the 2% early-payment discount and deducting TDS. All data is fictional.

Goods issue values the drums at the ₹1,450 standard cost, so the sale earns ₹19,40,000 − ₹14,50,000 = ₹4,90,000 gross margin (25.3%). Blue lines are debits and orange lines are credits.

Rule · SD–FI integration

  1. Assign the sales area before master data. A customer can't be extended to IN10/10/00, and an order can't be delivered from IN11, until the sales org belongs to IN10 and the plant is assigned in OVX6.
  2. VKOA before the first billing. If KOFI finds no account, billing doesn't fail. It saves the invoice and quietly leaves it out of accounting (O2C-09). I check VFX3 daily during go-live so nothing sits there unbilled in FI.
  3. FSV before the first goods issue. Part 5's expense items cover 511000–519999 and, across several items, 611000–679999, so 531000 would land in Not assigned and the P&L would stop balancing with no error. Following rule INS3-01, I added item Changes in inventories of finished goods (PL.EXP.CHI) with range 0000531000–0000539999 in OB58 and re-ran the check: 0 not assigned. 171200 needed no change, because Part 2 already added 0000171100–0000171999.

SAP Credit Management · UKM_BP · UKM_MY_DCDS

Credit check on the sales order

When the order is saved, SAP adds its value (including GST) to what the customer already owes and compares the total with the credit limit. A second rule blocks any customer whose oldest open item is more than 60 days overdue, however much room is left. A blocked order is saved but can't be delivered until the credit manager releases it.

F-28 · OBB8 · OBA3 · OBA4

Incoming payment: discount, TDS and differences

  1. DiscountTerms IN02 give 2% if paid within 10 days of the invoice (by 15.10.2026). OBY6 is set so the discount base is the net value, not the GST-inclusive total: 2% × ₹19,40,000 = ₹38,800.
  2. TDSKalinga's own turnover is above ₹10 crore and it has bought more than ₹50 lakh from IN10 this year, so as the buyer it deducts TDS under 194Q: 0.1% × ₹19,40,000 = ₹1,940. IN10 books it to 162000 and claims it against its own income tax once it shows in Form 26AS.
  3. ToleranceWhat's left over is compared with the smaller of ₹500 and 0.5% of the invoice (OBA3 group IN10). Within it, F-28 writes the gap off to 664000 (O2C-12, ₹250 bank charge). Above it, the invoice is cleared and a residual item stays open (O2C-11).
  4. AuthorityThe receipt clears a ₹22,89,200 customer item. AR clerk CLERK01's OBA4 limits are ₹5,00,000 per document and ₹2,00,000 per customer item, so large receipts are posted by MGR01 (O2C-10).

O2C-11: Kalinga pays on day 15 but still deducts ₹38,800. SAP allows no discount after day 10, so ₹38,800 is left as a residual item (PK 06) with reference to the original invoice. The AR team then decides: chase it, or approve a credit note if sales agrees to honour the late discount.

Business impact

Why O2C controls matter

Receivables are IN10's biggest current asset: ₹64.8 lakh, more than its cash and fixed deposit together. The credit check stops new sales to customers who are already stretched or seriously late, before the goods leave the plant. Delivery-related billing means nothing is invoiced that wasn't shipped, and nothing shipped goes unbilled. Early-payment discounts turn a 30-day receivable into a 9-day one at a known cost (₹38,800 on this invoice). Tolerances clear the small differences automatically and put the large ones in front of a person, and the ageing list tells the collections team exactly where to call.

How I present it in 90 seconds

  2. Integration: "Sales org IN10 posts to company code IN10. Goods issue uses OBYC, so stock is credited and COGS debited; billing uses VKOA for revenue and discounts, and OB40 for output GST."
  3. Controls: "SAP Credit Management blocks orders over the limit or with items 60 days overdue, OB52 and the MM period guard the dates, and OBA3 and OBA4 decide what F-28 can clear automatically."
  4. Proof: "Twelve unit tests and a simulator. And it ties back: the customer open items at 30 September add up to exactly the ₹64,80,000 of receivables in the trial balance of my month-end close (Part 5)."
Which O2C steps create an accounting entry?

Goods issue (Dr COGS, Cr stock), billing (Dr customer, Cr revenue and output tax) and the payment (Dr bank, Cr customer). The sales order and the delivery don't post to FI.

What is VKOA and what does it depend on?

Revenue account determination for billing. With condition type KOFI it looks up the chart of accounts, sales org, customer account assignment group, material account assignment group and account key (ERL revenue, ERS deductions) and returns a G/L account.

A billing document isn't in FI. What do you check?

VFX3 shows billing documents blocked for accounting and the reason. Most often it's account determination (a missing account assignment group, or a missing VKOA entry), a closed posting period or a missing cost center. Fix the cause and release it from VFX3.

How does the credit check work in S/4HANA?

SAP Credit Management: the BP has a credit segment with a limit and a risk class. On saving an order, the check rule adds the order value to the open receivables and open orders and blocks if the limit is exceeded or if an item is overdue beyond the set days. Blocked orders are released in UKM_MY_DCDS (VKM1 in older systems).

Why IGST for one customer and CGST + SGST for another?

GST follows the place of supply. The plant is in Maharashtra; a Maharashtra customer is intra-state (CGST + SGST), an Odisha customer is inter-state (IGST). The pricing procedure picks the condition from the regions.

What happens if a customer pays short?

F-28 compares the gap with the tolerance (the lower of the OBA3 customer group and the user's OBA4 limits). Within it, the difference is written off automatically; above it, you post a residual or partial payment, or leave it on account.
